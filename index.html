About

Good businesses stay hidden for fixable reasons.

44 Management is a publicity and visibility agency working with businesses across Nigeria. We exist because plenty of good businesses go unnoticed. Usually the product isn’t the problem. Nobody has worked out why people aren’t seeing it.

We don’t start by selling you a package. We start by finding the actual publicity problem: an inactive page, ads reaching the wrong people, no trust online, or no presence on the street. Then we apply the solution that fits.

How we work

  2. Identify

    Find the real problem

    We look at where you’re invisible: your pages, ads, reviews, how fast you reply and whether people can spot you on the street. You get a clear list of what’s actually wrong.

  3. Choose

    Pick only what fixes it

    We recommend the channels that solve those problems and nothing else. If you don’t need a new website or a billboard, we’ll tell you.

  4. Execute

    Run it, measure, adjust

    We do the work, report what’s working in plain language, and adjust as we learn. You always know where your money is going.

What you can expect

  • Honest adviceEven when the answer is “you don’t need much”.
  • No bloated packagesYou only pay for the work that solves your problem.
  • Plain-English reportsClear updates on what’s working and what we’re changing.

Services

Seven ways we get you seen.

Each service fixes a specific problem. We recommend only the ones you need. Tap any service to see what’s included.

Social Media Management

Managing your social media pages, content planning, posting and keeping a consistent online presence.

Fixes: inactive or inconsistent pages.

Signs you need it

  • Your last post was weeks ago
  • You post with no plan or theme
  • Plenty of likes, very few enquiries

What we do

  • A monthly content plan built around your goals
  • Design, captions and scheduling
  • Replying to comments and messages on your posts
  • A monthly report in plain English

Media Buying & Social Media Marketing

Paid advertising and social media campaigns that increase awareness and reach the right audience.

Fixes: great content that nobody sees.

Signs you need it

  • You boost posts with little to show for it
  • Your ads reach the wrong people or places
  • You can’t tell what your ad spend brings back

What we do

  • Audience and location targeting
  • Ad visuals and copy tested against each other
  • Budget management across the platforms that fit you
  • Clear reporting on what each result costs

Website Development

Websites and web applications built by our development team.

Fixes: no credible home online.

Signs you need it

  • No website, or one you’d rather not share
  • It’s slow, broken or hard to use on a phone
  • Visitors can’t work out how to buy, book or contact you

What we do

  • Fast, mobile-first websites and landing pages
  • Booking, ordering or enquiry flows
  • Web applications through our development team
  • Basic search setup so people can find you

Graphic Design

Social media designs, promotional graphics, campaign creatives and other visual materials.

Fixes: a brand that looks forgettable.

Signs you need it

  • Every post looks different
  • Your designs look homemade next to competitors
  • No brand kit or templates to work from

What we do

  • Social media designs and templates
  • Campaign and promotional graphics
  • Flyers, posters and print-ready files
  • A simple brand kit: colours, fonts and logo use

Online Customer Service & Support

Managing customer inquiries, responses and support across your online channels.

Fixes: unanswered messages and lost sales.

Signs you need it

  • Messages sit unanswered for hours
  • Enquiries get lost across WhatsApp, Instagram and email
  • You answer the same questions again and again

What we do

  • Managing replies across your online channels
  • Saved replies and FAQs
  • Handling enquiries, orders and complaints
  • A weekly summary of what customers are asking

Influencer Marketing

Finding the right influencers and creators, connecting them with your brand and managing the campaign.

Fixes: no trusted voices talking about you.

Signs you need it

  • Nobody outside your circle is talking about you
  • You’ve paid influencers before with nothing to show
  • You’re not sure which creators fit your brand

What we do

  • Finding creators whose audience matches yours
  • Briefs, negotiation and agreements
  • Managing content and posting schedules
  • Tracking reach and results per creator

Outdoor Marketing

Billboards, posters, flyers and other offline advertising.

Fixes: invisible in your own neighbourhood.

Signs you need it

  • People walk past without noticing you
  • You’re opening a new location or launching locally
  • Your customers are offline as much as online

What we do

  • Billboard and poster planning
  • Flyer design, printing and distribution
  • Choosing locations based on footfall
  • Matching your street presence to your online look
